05 / TECH QUIZ

Play fair.
Think fast.

Clear rules keep the challenge exciting, balanced and focused on teamwork.

01

Team of 4

A fast-paced technical quiz designed to test knowledge, teamwork, accuracy and quick decision-making.

02

Quiz format

  • Each team consists of 4 members.
  • One designated member submits the team's answers.
  • The other 3 members may discuss and suggest answers within the given time.
  • Each question has a fixed time limit.
  • Teams must answer every question within the allotted time.
  • Accuracy and response time may be considered for scoring.
03

Team rules

  • Teams must participate using one designated device.
  • Team members must remain together throughout the quiz.
  • External assistance, internet searches, AI tools, notes and reference materials are strictly prohibited.
  • Once an answer is submitted, no changes will be permitted.
  • Any cheating or unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • In case of a tie, a tie-breaker round will be conducted.
  • The decision of the quiz coordinators will be final and binding.
